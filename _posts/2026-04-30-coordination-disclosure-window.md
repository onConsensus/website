We agreed with four sequencer operators that the substance of this
piece would be held until a defined disclosure window had closed. The
window closes on 15 December 2026; until then, the sealed commit
above is everything a reader is entitled to.

The mechanics of the seal are documented at
[/verify/#embargoes](/verify/#embargoes). After the embargo lifts, the
sealed sha-256 of this article's source must match the hash a reader
recomputes against the post markdown. If those values diverge, the
text has changed since seal — either we corrected an error and noted
it, or something is wrong, and the [corrections log](/corrections/)
will say which.
