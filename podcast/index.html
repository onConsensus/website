Every On Consensus article ships with a build-time narration. Subscribe in any podcast client by pasting one of the feed URLs below. Authors may replace the synthetic narration with a hand-recorded edition by setting audio_override: in the article's frontmatter.

Combined feed

  • /podcast.xml — everything On Consensus publishes.

Per-section feeds

  • /podcast/governance.xml — Governance. DAOs, slashing, voting, and the politics of protocols.
  • /podcast/research.xml — Research. Original analysis and notes from the academic edge.
  • /podcast/development.xml — Development. Implementation reports, post-mortems, and notes from node operators.
  • /podcast/cryptography.xml — Cryptography. Primitives, proofs, and the math underneath the chains.
  • /podcast/culture.xml — Culture. The people, the prose, and the politics of decentralization.
  • /podcast/dispatches.xml — Dispatches. Short-form link-blog. On the wire.
