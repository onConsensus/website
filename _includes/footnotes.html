{%- comment -%} footnotes.html — markup scaffold for the marginalia rail. Kramdown emits `[^n]` footnotes inline as a `.footnotes` block at the end of the rendered article body. The Reading Experience task wires the JS that *moves* those `
  • ` items into this rail on desktop (sidebar) and rolls them into an accordion on mobile. This include is the empty container the JS will hydrate into; it is safe to render even when a post has no footnotes — it stays empty. {%- endcomment -%}
